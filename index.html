FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know about working with me.

What is your typical turnaround time?
Turnaround time depends on project size. Small projects (under 500 entries) are typically delivered within 24-48 hours. Larger projects are completed based on agreed timelines with regular progress updates.
How do you ensure data accuracy?
I use a multi-step validation process including automated checks, cross-referencing, and manual review. My current accuracy rate is 99.9%, and I offer free revisions for any errors found.
What file formats do you support?
I work with all major formats including Excel (.xlsx), Google Sheets, CSV, PDF data extraction, Word documents, and can input directly into CRMs like Salesforce, HubSpot, and Zoho.
Is my data kept confidential?
Absolutely. I sign NDAs when required and follow strict data handling protocols. All client data is deleted from my systems within 7 days of project completion unless otherwise agreed.
Do you offer bulk discounts?
Yes! I offer discounted rates for ongoing contracts and large-volume projects. Contact me for a custom quote based on your specific requirements and volume.
